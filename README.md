# 🙏 Theravada Stories

A curated public repository of Buddhist stories from the Theravada tradition, dedicated to preserving and sharing the wisdom of early Buddhism with the world.

## 📖 About This Repository

Theravada Stories is a digital depository of authentic Buddhist narratives, parables, and teachings rooted in the Theravada tradition. These stories serve as timeless guides for spiritual practice, ethical living, and the pursuit of enlightenment through the Dhamma (Buddhist teachings).

This repository makes these sacred stories freely accessible to anyone seeking to deepen their understanding of Buddhist philosophy and practice, regardless of geographical location or economic circumstance.

## 🌟 Purpose

- **Preserve** authentic Theravada Buddhist stories and teachings
- **Share** Buddhist wisdom with the global community
- **Inspire** spiritual practitioners of all levels
- **Educate** about the teachings of the Buddha (Dhamma)

## 📜 Story Categories

Stories in this repository include:

- **Jataka Tales** - Stories of the Buddha's previous lives
- **Dhammapada Stories** - Tales illustrating Buddhist verses
- **Avadana Stories** - Narratives of Buddhist masters and disciples
- **Paritta** - Protective discourses and blessings
- **Historical Narratives** - Stories from Buddhist texts and traditions
- **Wisdom Tales** - Stories about virtue, compassion, and mindfulness

## 🕉️ The Theravada Tradition

Theravada (literally "Way of the Elders") is the oldest surviving school of Buddhism, preserving the earliest teachings of the Buddha. The Theravada tradition emphasizes:

- Direct study of the Pali Canon (original Buddhist scriptures)
- The path to individual enlightenment (Nirvana)
- Monastic practice and scholarship
- Mindfulness and meditation
